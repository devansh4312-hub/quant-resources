How the run works
Chips — every solve is a playing card. Its rank comes from difficulty (2 → A) and its suit from the platform (Codeforces ♠, LeetCode ♥, CSES ♣, AtCoder ♦). Higher ranks score more chips.
Mult — starts at 1, +1 per streak day, +2 per contest solve this round, plus the bonuses of your 5 equipped Jokers. ×Mult Jokers multiply on top. Click any unlocked Joker to equip or unequip it.
Money — $1 per solve, $3 per Small Blind (daily goal), $4 per Big Blind (weekly goal), $5 per Boss Blind, plus $1 interest per 3 streak days (max $5).
Arcana Packs — spend $4 to draw up to 3 problems you marked ↺ revisit or solved shakily. Pick one as your Tarot; finish the redo for +$2.
Antes — each round runs Mon–Sun. Every round you beat the Big Blind raises your Ante. The Boss Blind rotates weekly with a new challenge.
Poker hands — the cards you solve on the same day form a hand: two of one rank is a Pair, five consecutive ranks a Straight, five of one suit a Flush. Many Jokers unlock from hands.
Editions — starred solves are Foil, contest solves are Polychrome, and ↺ revisit cards are debuffed until you redo them.
